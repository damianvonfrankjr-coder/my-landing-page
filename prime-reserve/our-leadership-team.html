Meet the leadership team

Engineers and accountants holding Reserve Specialist credentials through the Community Associations Institute — the people who inspect, model, and write every study.

David Whann Jr.

Chief Executive Officer

MBA · RS #369 · EBP

Connect on LinkedIn →

David graduated from the University of South Florida with a bachelor’s in Accounting and an MBA, and brings ten years in the capital planning and reserve study industry. Starting as an intern, he advanced to acting national Director of Operations at his prior company, where he completed over 500 reserve studies nationwide — including Structural Integrity Reserve Studies in Florida.

His experience spans HOAs, industrial storage, high-rise condominiums, office complexes, museums, country clubs, marinas, and private islands. He has created educational materials, overseen internal systems, trained project managers, established quality standards across regional offices, reviewed over 100 reserve studies, and presented at virtual webinars and the 2025 CAI Conference.

David co-founded Prime Reserve Planning to provide greater value through high-quality, tailored reserve study reports and excellent customer service.

Jacob Conley

Chief Financial Officer

RS #503 · EBP · Civil Engineering, Trine University

Connect on LinkedIn →

Jacob graduated from Trine University with a degree in Civil Engineering and has over four years in the capital planning and reserve study industry. A co-founder of Prime Reserve Planning, he has completed over 250 reserve studies across the United States — for HOAs, low-rise to high-rise condominiums, assisted living facilities, country clubs, timeshare and resort properties, and worship facilities.

He has developed specialized tools and worksheets, trained coworkers on timeshare reserve studies, and regularly presents findings to boards. A construction industry background gives him practical insight into building systems and maintenance planning, and he specializes in translating complex funding plans into understandable formats.

His customer service philosophy: “Once a client, always a client.”

Shawn Conover

Chief Operating Officer

RS #504 · EIT · EBP · Civil Engineering, Trine University

Connect on LinkedIn →

Shawn graduated from Trine University with a degree in Civil Engineering and a minor in Structural Engineering, and has over four years in the capital planning and reserve study industry. A co-founder of Prime Reserve Planning, he has completed over 250 reserve studies for commercial facilities, HOAs, high-rise office and condominium buildings, country clubs, and resort-style timeshare developments nationwide.

Shawn believes reserve studies help communities plan decades ahead — translating complex planning into practical strategies that preserve infrastructure, maintain property values, and build financially stable communities.
