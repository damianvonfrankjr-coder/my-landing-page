Property types we serve

Customized solutions that assist our clients in planning for long-term maintenance, protecting their investments, and confidently achieving their financial goals.

Business & office buildings

Strategies for maintaining financial health in commercial properties — helping business owners plan for long-term capital improvements and ensure a well-maintained environment for tenants.

Condominium associations
Detailed reserve studies that assist condominium associations in managing shared expenses, planning for future repairs, and promoting transparency among members.

Country clubs & golf courses

Enhanced financial planning for recreational properties, ensuring clubs can maintain high standards for facilities and services while managing shared costs effectively.

Homeowners associations (HOAs)
Well-maintained communal spaces and long-term financial planning strategies, so residents enjoy a thriving community without unexpected costs.

Municipalities

Capital Reserve Plans customized for public entities — town halls, office spaces, public libraries, historic buildings, maintenance complexes, parks, and more. Each entity has different needs based on size and complexity, and we work with any and all of them for the best report value.

New developments
Essential planning for properties under construction, equipping developers and future associations with the financial insights needed to maintain shared amenities and preserve value.

Schools & universities

Financial guidance that helps educational institutions secure their shared spaces — so they can focus on providing quality education while maintaining facilities sustainably.

Senior & assisted living facilities
Managing shared costs and maintenance needs for multifamily dwellings, keeping property owners competitive while providing a comfortable living experience for residents.

Timeshares & vacation ownership

Customized financial strategies that help owners navigate the complexities of shared ownership and maintain the value of their investments.

Townhome communities
Tailored reserve studies addressing the specific financial needs of townhome associations, promoting proactive maintenance and community engagement.

Worship facilities

Reserve studies for churches, mosques, synagogues, and temples — sustainable facility management that lets congregations focus on their mission without financial worries.
